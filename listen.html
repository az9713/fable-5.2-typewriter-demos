Report

This text contains your answers and the measured numbers. Copy it and send it.

How the three numbers are measured

  • Noise level: the device plays a silent input. The page compares that level with the music part of the device output.
  • Bandwidth at −6 dB: the device plays a white noise probe. The limits are the frequencies where the level is 6 dB below the median level of the pass band.
  • Speed variation: the device plays a probe tone. The number is the largest change of the tone frequency, as a percentage.
